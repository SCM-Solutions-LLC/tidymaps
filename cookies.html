TidyMap sets no cookies at all. Not for analytics, not for advertising, not for sign-in. What it does use is local storage — a few keys kept in your own browser, on your own device, listed in full below.

The difference, briefly

A cookie is sent to a server with every request you make. Local storage is not: it stays on your device until something clears it, and our servers only see a value if the app deliberately puts it in a request. Nothing on this page is ever attached to a request automatically.

Everything TidyMap keeps in your browser

  • tidymap_draft_v2 — your work in progress: the space you are planning, your answers, and the plan once it exists. This is what lets you refresh the page, or come back later, without starting over. If you are not signed in, this is the only place your plan lives. Cleared by “Start over.”
  • tidymap_units — whether you measure in inches or centimeters, so the app does not ask twice.
  • tidymap_anon_v1 — a random identifier for anonymous usage counts, so we can tell one visit from another without knowing who you are. It is not linked to your name, email, photos, or IP address, and it is never sent to a third party. This is the only non-essential item on the list, and it is never created at all if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.
  • tidymap_invite_email — the email address you typed into the early-access form, kept so the form remembers you asked. Only written if you use that form.
  • sb-…-auth-token — your sign-in session, written by Supabase's authentication library. This is what keeps you signed in between visits. It only exists if you sign in, and signing out removes it.

That is the complete list. If you find a key in your browser under this site that is not named here, we want to know: contact@scmsolutions.org.

Cleaning up after ourselves

An early version of TidyMap asked you to paste in your own AI API key, and stored it in this browser. That version is gone, and the app now deletes both of those old keys automatically the first time you load it, telling you it has done so. We mention it because if you used TidyMap long ago, something of yours was removed without you asking, and you should hear that from us.

No third-party tracking

  • No Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no advertising or attribution SDK of any kind.
  • Usage events go to our own database, not to an analytics company.
  • Fonts are served from this site, not from Google Fonts, so loading a page does not tell anyone else you were here.
  • The only outbound requests a page makes are to our own backend, and to a retailer's site if you choose to tap a product link.

Why there is no consent banner

Because there is nothing to consent to that a banner would meaningfully change. There are no cookies, no third-party trackers, and no data sold or shared for advertising. Everything in the list above is either doing a job you asked for — remembering your plan, keeping you signed in — or is the anonymous counter, which switches itself off when your browser asks it to.

If you would rather not be counted, turn on Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track in your browser and TidyMap will stop collecting usage events entirely, on this device, with no further action from you.

Clearing all of it

  • Start over in the app clears your draft plan.
  • Sign out removes your session.
  • Clearing site data for this site in your browser removes everything on this page in one go. You will lose an unsaved plan, so save or export it first if it matters.
  • Using a private or incognito window means everything here disappears when you close it. TidyMap works there — it just cannot remember anything between visits.

If your browser blocks site storage entirely, TidyMap still runs. You will just be starting fresh each time.
