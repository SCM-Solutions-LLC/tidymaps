# Surface brief: index.html (whole-site world; landing is the first surface)

Scope: Persuade. Visitor: a parent or renter on a phone in front of one cluttered space, mid-week. Job: believe the app reads their photos and returns a plan they can start tonight for $0, then tap Plan my space. Proof on hand: the sample pantry plan, product art, the 3D view. No testimonials or counts exist; none may be invented. Constraints: buildless static site, self-hosted fonts only, WCAG AA, existing copy kept where tests bind it, wizard flow and backend untouched.

Chosen direction: The Home-Economics Manual. Memorable moment: Fig. 1, an inked pantry elevation whose jumbled items slide into labeled zones.

Cross-surface reach: wizard steps read as Lesson n of 11 with figure-numbered diagrams; results are the Plan chapter with figure tables and printed stage marks per step; products page is the Appendix of suppliers; dashboard is the workbook contents page; legal pages are back-matter notes.

Unresolved: whether the 3D viewer keeps a place in the first viewport (it does not; it stays a figure inside the plan).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A plan is a lesson. Numbered figures teach the shelf; the exercise box is the button. Refuses the photo-left, headline-right, three-feature-cards arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: White stock, one turquoise spot ink laid at page scale, ink black, a single 18% halftone tint. No grey, no gradients, corners 0 to 2px, 1px black rules. Archivo for chapter titles (expanded, black weight) and caps labels (narrow); Source Serif 4 for reading. Figures are hand-inked cupboard elevations captioned "Fig. n". States are printed marks (empty, half, filled square), never hue.

STORY: Sees a mess become zoned in Fig. 1, reads that photos are applied against household rules, starts the wizard.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Turquoise band across the top holding the chapter number at display scale and the headline as chapter title; beneath, Fig. 1 full width (inked SVG elevation, items animate from jumble to zones, leader lines draw in); under the figure, the exercise box: a ruled frame with Plan my space and View a sample plan.

FORM: Home-Economics Manual, 4th of 7 on the ordered list, seed db34b2de. Signature interaction: the Fig. 1 sort. Motion grammar: inked in one stroke, exponential ease-out, reduced motion shows the finished figure.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
