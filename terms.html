What TidyMap is

You send photos or a short video of one space in your home, answer some questions about it, and an AI model writes you an organization plan: where things should go, a checklist of moves, rough time estimates, and sometimes storage products worth considering. That is the whole service.

TidyMap is free while it is in early access. We are not charging for it, and there is nothing to cancel. If we ever introduce a paid tier, we will not start billing an existing account without asking you first.

A plan is a suggestion, not professional advice

This is the most important thing on this page, so it goes near the top.

TidyMap's plans are generated by an AI model looking at photos. They can be wrong. The model can misjudge a shelf's depth, miss a hazard that is out of frame, misread how much weight a bracket holds, or suggest a bin that does not fit. Treat every plan as a starting point from someone who has not stood in your room.

You are responsible for doing the work safely. Before you mount, anchor, stack, or climb anything: check it yourself. Anchoring shelving and tall furniture to studs, weight limits, ladder safety, storing chemicals and medication away from children and pets, and anything touching electrical, plumbing, gas, or structure are yours to get right — and are worth a licensed professional when you are unsure.

TidyMap is not a home inspection, not a contractor, not a safety audit, and not medical, legal, or financial advice. Measurements shown in a plan are estimates read off photos, not a survey. Measure the real space before you buy anything. A plan mentioning a product is not a promise it will fit, be in stock, or cost what a retailer listed yesterday.
